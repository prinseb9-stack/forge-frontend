Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data FORGE ("we", "us") collects, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

Account data: when you sign up, we collect your email address and a Firebase user ID. Authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication.

Connected platform data: when you connect a platform (e.g. Bluesky, TikTok), we receive and store:

  • Your username / handle and display name on that platform
  • Your avatar URL (if the platform provides one)
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens, encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM

Content data: prompts and generated content you create in FORGE, plus any scheduled posts.

Usage data: your plan tier and generation counts (used for quota enforcement).

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate you and maintain your session
  • To generate content using AI providers (Groq, Agnes AI)
  • To publish or schedule content to platforms you have connected
  • To enforce plan limits and process payments

3. What We Do NOT Do

  • We do not sell your data
  • We do not share your tokens with third parties
  • We do not read your connected platform's private messages or non-public data
  • We do not log access tokens, refresh tokens, or passwords

4. Data Storage and Security

Data is stored in Google Firestore. OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM. Encryption keys are stored as environment secrets separate from the data itself.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You can:

  • Disconnect a platform at any time from the Platforms page. This deletes the stored tokens for that platform immediately.
  • Delete your account by contacting support@forgeai.studio. We will delete all your data within 30 days.

6. Third-Party Services

FORGE integrates with:

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication and database
  • Groq — AI text generation
  • Agnes AI — AI image generation
  • Flutterwave — payment processing
  • Bluesky, TikTok, and other social platforms — only when you connect them

7. Children

FORGE is not intended for users under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction).

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy. The "Last updated" date above will reflect changes.

9. Contact

Privacy questions: support@forgeai.studio
